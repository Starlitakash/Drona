# ACME CORP HR POLICIES & EMPLOYEE BENEFITS

## 1. Paid Time Off (PTO) & Leave Accrual
- Full-time employees accrue 22 days of paid time off per calendar year (1.83 days per month).
- A maximum of 5 unused PTO days can be rolled over to the following year. Any unused days beyond 5 are forfeited on December 31.
- Leave requests exceeding 3 consecutive business days must be submitted through the BambooHR portal at least 14 days in advance.

## 2. Parental & Family Leave
- Primary caregivers are eligible for 16 weeks of 100% paid parental leave following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child.
- Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks of 100% paid leave.
- Parental leave must be taken within the first 12 months of the child's arrival and can be taken continuously or in two separate blocks.

## 3. Health & Wellness Benefits
- **Health Insurance:** Choice between BlueCross PPO (Gold plan) and Kaiser HMO. The company covers 90% of employee premiums and 75% of dependent premiums.
- **Mental Health:** Employees receive 12 free therapy sessions per year through Spring Health.
- **Annual Wellness Stipend:** $500 per calendar year for gym memberships, fitness equipment, or wellness apps. Claims must be submitted with receipts by November 30.

## 4. Remote Work & Core Collaboration Hours
- Employees are permitted up to 3 remote work days per week (Mondays and Fridays are standard remote days).
- Core working hours across all global teams are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM Eastern Time. Team members must maintain active Slack presence during core hours.
