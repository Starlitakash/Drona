# ACME CORP TRAVEL & EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

## 1. General Expense Guidelines & Approvals
- Expenses under $150 (books, home office accessories, minor software subscriptions) do not require prior approval.
- Expenses between $150 and $1,000 require written approval from your direct Team Manager via Slack or email.
- Expenses exceeding $1,000 require approval from the Department Vice President and Finance Lead.
- All expense reimbursement claims must be submitted via Expensify within 30 days of purchase with itemized receipts attached.

## 2. Business Travel & Per-Diem Rates
- **Flights:** Economy class is standard for all domestic and short-haul flights under 6 hours. Business class is permitted for international flights exceeding 6 hours with VP approval.
- **Lodging:** Maximum hotel allowance is $250/night for Tier 1 cities (NYC, SF, London, Tokyo) and $180/night for all other locations.
- **Daily Meal Per-Diem:** A flat rate of $75/day ($15 breakfast, $25 lunch, $35 dinner). Alcohol is not reimbursable unless during an approved client entertainment dinner.

## 3. Home Office & Tech Equipment Setup
- New hires receive a one-time $800 home office setup stipend to purchase ergonomic chairs, monitors, or desks.
- Standard company equipment provided by IT: 16-inch MacBook Pro (or ThinkPad X1 Carbon for Linux/Windows users), 1x 27-inch 4K monitor, and YubiKey security keys.
- Lost or damaged equipment must be reported immediately to IT Support via the `#it-helpdesk` Slack channel.
